About

I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at North Carolina State University, advised by Dr. Min Chi. My research focuses on reinforcement learning, generative AI, and LLM post-training. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University, where I was advised by Dr. Philipp Koehn. Previously, I earned a BSc (Honours) from Oxford Brookes University and a B.Eng. from Chengdu University of Technology, both in Software Engineering, through the Sino-British Collaborative Education Programme, where I was advised by Joojo Walker. I graduated with First Class Honours, ranking in the top 1% of my cohort.
