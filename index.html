About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at North Carolina State University, advised by Prof. Min Chi. Previously, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University, where I was advised by Prof. Philipp Koehn. In 2024, I was also fortunate to work with Prof. Hua Xu as a research assistant at Tsinghua University.

Research Interests

My research interests span reinforcement learning, generative AI, and the post-training of large language models.

  • Reinforcement Learning

    Learning robust decision-making policies from imperfect human demonstrations, with a focus on apprenticeship learning, inverse reinforcement learning, and sequential decisions in education and healthcare.

  • Generative AI

    Developing generative models that reason, assess, and interact with people to support learning and clinical decision-making, with an emphasis on reliability and uncertainty.

  • LLM Post-Training

    Understanding and shaping how language models express preferences through preference optimization, mechanistic analysis, and efficient control of internal representations.
