About

I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at North Carolina State University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Dr. Min Chi. Prior to that, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University, advised by Dr. Philipp Koehn. I was also fortunate to work with Prof. Hua Xu as a research assistant at the Tsinghua University in 2024.

Research Interests

My research interests include:

  • Reinforcement Learning: Learning from imperfect demonstrations through imitation learning, inverse RL, and reward modeling.
  • Generative AI: Cross-lingual transfer, multimodal learning, and applications in education and healthcare.
  • LLM Post-Training: Preference optimization, alignment, and interpretable control of model behavior.
